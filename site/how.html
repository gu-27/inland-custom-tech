How I help

Tools, talks, and transformation.

Three ways to work together, from a quick setup to a change that lasts. Pick the one that fits, or start with a free call and we will figure it out.

Tools

Things I build or set up for you, ready to use and yours to keep.

  • Rebrand and website. A brand and a site that say who you are, with the simple tools behind it.
  • AI starter kit. Set up and comfortable with AI, customized to how you work.
  • Personal or company brain. Your notes, meetings, and past work in one place you own, so AI answers from what you know.
  • Automations. The repetitive tasks in your week, set up to run on their own.
  • Website audit. A clear report on what is slow, what breaks on phones, and where a visitor gets lost, with what to fix first.
  • Dashboards. Scattered numbers pulled onto one clear page.
  • Privacy tools. Ways to use AI without handing over personal details.

Talks

One session for your team, class, or event. An easy first step.

  • Mindful technology use. Using tools on purpose, and noticing when they use you.
  • AI, used well. Useful, honest, and still human.
  • Tool overviews. What is out there, what it does, and what it is worth.
  • Strategy sessions. Where technology fits in your plans, worked out together.
  • Curiosity and exploration. Hands-on time to try things, ask questions, and get unstuck.

Transformation

Longer work alongside your team, for change that holds after I leave.

  • Training built around your people. Concepts learned through what people care about, then skills built on their real work.
  • A culture of curious problem solvers. Norms, environments, and experiences where growth through technology is expected and valued.
  • Technology strategy. A clear plan for the tools you have and the ones you need, and a team ready to use them.
