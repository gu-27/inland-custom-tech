In progress · Marketing

A marketing pipeline

For a student-led organization with a lot to say and a small team saying it. The goal: post with a plan, not just to stay busy.

  1. Set the strategy. The brand, who we are talking to, one core message, and the channels that reach them.
  2. Make the plan. Goals, a calendar, who does what, and how we will know it is working.
  3. Run the work. Campaigns, plus custom tools like an open idea board and content workflows, so posts go out with a plan and better quality.

Built with Claude, GitHub, Cloudflare and a shared project board.

In progress · Knowledge

A company brain

For a small family company whose know-how lived in one person's head, a few inboxes, and a lot of old files.

  1. Talk to it anywhere. Voice while you drive, a message from your phone, or typing at your laptop. Each one adds to it and gets answers back.
  2. It remembers. Notes, meetings, and past work live in one private place the company owns, sorted so anyone can find them.
  3. It helps run the work. The next step connects it to customer inquiries, scheduling, follow-ups, invoicing, and marketing, in both directions.

Built with Claude and a private GitHub repository.

Ready to set up · Family

A family planner

For families who want kids to get organized on their own. One-time setup, no monthly fees, and no app to download.

  1. One-time setup. I give you the prompt and the step-by-step instructions. No monthly fees, ever.
  2. Each kid builds their own. Goals, music, what they need and want, what they want to learn, rewards and their own colors. Getting organized becomes theirs.
  3. Share a link. It opens on any phone with no app to download and nothing tracking them. You control the login, it runs on your email, the data stays with you, and you can change it anytime.

A simple website you own. The sky is the limit on what goes in it.

Training · Culture

Train and build your culture

Built from years of teaching young adults to grow with technology, coaching teams, consulting with startups and building my own, and designing and running curriculum. All of it with one purpose: helping people grow through experience.

Part 1 · Training

Training built around your people

Learning that starts with what people already care about and ends with skills they use at work.

  1. Learn it your way. The same concept, delivered through what each person cares about: their interests, audio, visuals, in person or on their own time.
  2. Build the skill. Exercises from their real work, hands-on labs in their own tools, and problems that get harder as they go.
  3. Keep going. People leave curious enough to learn what is new and practical enough to put it to work.
Part 2 · Culture

A culture of curious problem solvers

For organizations that want people to keep growing with technology long after a training ends.

  1. Set the norms. Understand the big picture of your company, technology, and growth, and agree on how growth is supported.
  2. Create the environments. Places where growth is experienced through technology and valued, and an error is part of learning, not a failure.
  3. Grow the culture. A culture of curious problem solvers, growing through technology and through your company.

More on the way: video editing, websites, AI 101 training. Each gets added here once it ships.
