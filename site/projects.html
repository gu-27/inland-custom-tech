In progress · Marketing

A marketing engine

For a student-led organization with a lot to say and a small team saying it. The goal: post with a plan, not just to stay busy.

  1. Strategy first. Who we're talking to, what we want each audience to feel, and one core message.
  2. An open idea board. Anyone can add an idea. One person approves. Ideas move from vetted, to developed, to the calendar.
  3. Reach, then hold. Short hooks go out as Reels to reach new people. Pinned posts and highlights hold the story for anyone who looks closer.

Built with Claude, GitHub, Cloudflare and a shared project board.

In progress · Knowledge

A company brain

For a small family company whose know-how lived in one person's head, a few inboxes, and a lot of old files.

  1. Gather it. Notes, documents, meeting recaps and voice memos go into one place.
  2. Organize it. It lives in a private repository, sorted into ideas, a running log, and an inbox for anything new. Plain files the company owns.
  3. Ask it. Anyone on the team asks a question in plain words. The answer points to the file it came from.

Built with Claude and a private GitHub repository.

Ready to set up · Family

A family planner

For families who want kids to get organized on their own. One-time setup, no monthly fees, and no app to download.

  1. One-time setup. I give you the prompt and the step-by-step instructions. No monthly fees, ever.
  2. Each kid builds their own. Goals, music, what they need and want, what they want to learn, rewards and their own colors. Getting organized becomes theirs.
  3. Share a link. It opens on any phone with no app to download and nothing tracking them. You control the login, it runs on your email, the data stays with you, and you can change it anytime.

A simple website you own. The sky is the limit on what goes in it.
