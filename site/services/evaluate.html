What it is

Evaluate looks at the people side. Who uses what, who quietly works around it, who is waiting to be told it is safe, and who is the real decision maker regardless of the org chart.

Most technology does not fail because the technology failed. It fails because the people side was never looked at. This is the step that looks at it.

Who it’s for

  • Larger organizations where the tools are already bought and adoption is the problem.
  • Any leader who has rolled something out and watched it not take.

What you walk away with

  • A read on how your team behaves around technology, in plain language.
  • The specific blockers, named. Usually some are structural and some are one conversation away from being solved.
  • A realistic view of how much change your organization can absorb at once.
