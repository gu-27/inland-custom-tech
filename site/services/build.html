What it is

Build is the part where something appears. A website, a dashboard, an automation, a small internal app, a tool that does the thing you have been doing by hand.

Built simply on purpose. Fewer moving parts means fewer things to break and a smaller bill later. Then it is installed, connected to your real work, and handed over.

Who it’s for

  • Small businesses who need one specific thing to exist.
  • Growing companies who need the thing to fit the way they already work, rather than the other way round.

What you walk away with

  • The working thing, in place, with your real data in it.
  • Plain documentation of how it works and how to change it.
  • Ownership. The code and the accounts are yours, and you are not locked to us.

Example

The follow-up tool this business runs on. An inquiry comes in through a form, AI drafts a reply, and nothing is sent until a person reads it and clicks approve. It runs on static hosting with no framework and no server to maintain, and the daily spend is capped so a bad day cannot become a bad bill.
