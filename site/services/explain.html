What it is

Explain is a working session about the landscape. What AI and automation can genuinely do right now, what is still a demo, and what applies to a business shaped like yours.

No slides full of acronyms. We talk about your actual work and sort it into things technology is good at, things it is bad at, and things that are not a technology problem at all.

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who keeps hearing they should be doing something with AI and cannot tell which parts are real.
  • Owners and leaders who have to make a decision and do not want to be sold to while they make it.

What you walk away with

  • A shared vocabulary, so the next conversation is faster.
  • A short list of what is worth trying, and an equally short list of what to ignore.
  • Permission to not do something. Often the most useful thing in the hour.
