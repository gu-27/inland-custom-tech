John CorreiaFounder, Inland Custom Tech
About

Curious by nature. Built to help you grow.

I started this company because I love figuring out problems, and technology is one of the best ways to solve them. I don’t show up with a packaged answer. I sit down, learn what you’re dealing with, and work out what would actually help.

My background covers two things that rarely sit together: how people learn and adopt technology, and the hands-on skill to build the tools. Most new technology fails because the people side is handled badly, not because of the technology. I work on both sides.

I work with solo operators, growing companies, nonprofits, and established organizations across Spokane, Coeur d’Alene, and the Inland Northwest.

Areas of experience

Interactive audio design

A high-quality working prototype that combines computer vision, computer engineering, audio engineering, audio design, and app development. Also a platform that captures sound at live venues and streams it back as something you can listen around in.

Patents

A patent on that same audio system, with a second patent pending.

Dashboards

Scattered numbers pulled into one picture, including a league-wide attendance study built from two public data feeds.

Automation

Repeat work taken off people’s plates. One tool drafts replies to new inquiries with AI and sends nothing until a person approves it.

Custom apps

From simple webpages, to small tools that solve one problem, to larger design systems. Each one built for the job in front of it.

Behavior and mindfulness

A dissertation on the behavioral side of problematic social media use, and how mindfulness can reduce it. Seven peer-reviewed papers on why people take up technology or work around it.

Privacy and security

Research on why people follow or ignore security practice, and on the privacy choices people make with fitness technology. Also a tool that strips personal information out of a document before anything reaches an AI.

Training

From the basics of technology up to the behavioral side of adoption, taught to people who start with no technical background.
